Privacy Policy

Divine Time — devotional home-screen widgets

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Divine Time ("the app") handles information when you use it. Divine Time is an Android app that offers devotional artwork and home-screen widgets featuring Hindu deities.

1. Information We Collect

Based on the developer's description, Divine Time currently has no app-operated backend and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. The app's widget artwork and designs are bundled with the app.

The app may store widget configuration or preferences locally on your device so your selected widgets can be displayed and updated. The final app build and its dependencies should be checked to confirm exactly what is stored and whether any included library processes information.

Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer, and other platform services may process information under their own policies. This policy does not make claims on behalf of those services.

2. How Information Is Used

Where widget configuration or preferences are stored locally, they are used to display and update your selected widget designs. According to the developer, the app does not send this configuration to a Divine Time backend.

3. Devotional Content and Artwork

Divine Time provides Hindu deity-themed illustrations and devotional home-screen widgets, including artwork depicting Hindu deities such as Ganesha, Krishna, Shiva, Vishnu, and Hanuman. This content is provided for devotional, cultural, and personalization purposes.

The app does not ask users to declare their religious beliefs as a condition of using these features. The presence of devotional content does not, by itself, mean that religious beliefs or other sensitive personal information are collected.

If future features allow users to submit personal information, transmit religious preferences off-device, or create accounts, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

4. Permissions and Background Operation

Android permissions may be used to support widget display, widget updates, or time-sensitive updates. The permissions requested depend on the final app build and Android version. The developer should verify the app's declared permissions and ensure that any permission requiring additional explanation is accurately described before release.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

Divine Time does not operate its own backend to receive user information. If you choose to share the app or content using another app on your device, the information you choose to share is handled by that app and its provider under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Third-Party Services

The developer has indicated that the app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Android, Google Play, and any third-party libraries included in the final build may process information as part of their operation. The app's final dependency list should be reviewed to confirm this section is accurate.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Widget settings stored locally may be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. The precise behavior depends on the app's implementation and Android version. Divine Time does not have an app-operated backend database for user records, according to the developer.
